Homepage Structure (Design Brief)

I want the homepage to feel premium, minimal, and modern, similar to tech consulting or SaaS companies. It should be clean, with plenty of white space, blue accents, and a professional feel.

The homepage should include the following sections in this order.



1\. Hero Section (Top of Homepage)

This is the first section visitors see.

Layout

Two-column layout.

Left Side

Main headline (pitch line)





Short supporting text





Primary button





Right Side

Professional photo of me(Drive Link Attached) Choose whichever background matches with theme





Headline Example

One-Stop Advertising Growth for E-commerce Brands

Supporting Text

One short sentence explaining what I do.

Example:

Expert-driven retail media advertising to help brands scale profitably

Button

Primary CTA button:

Get Your Free Audit
