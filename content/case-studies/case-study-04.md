Title: Unlocking Low-Hanging Revenue - Adding Hundreds of Thousands in Incremental Sales with High-Efficiency Catch-All Campaigns



One of the most consistently overlooked opportunities in Amazon advertising sits outside of core campaign structures.

Most accounts are optimized around primary keywords and high-intent traffic. While these campaigns drive the majority of revenue, they also operate in the most competitive auctions, where CPCs are elevated and efficiency is harder to maintain.

What often goes uncaptured is a secondary layer of demand:

&#x20;long-tail queries, loosely matched search behavior, and low-competition placements that are rarely targeted with intent.

This is where structured catch-all campaigns become a meaningful lever.

These campaigns are designed to expand coverage without introducing cost inefficiency. Rather than competing aggressively, they operate with controlled bid levels, allowing entry into lower-cost auctions that are typically ignored by standard campaign structures.

The objective is not volume through force, but volume through positioning.

Across multiple accounts, this approach consistently surfaces incremental demand that would otherwise remain untapped, delivering:

ACoS typically in the 5%–20% range

Significantly lower CPCs compared to primary campaigns

Incremental revenue in the hundreds of thousands

This layer operates independently of core campaigns, avoiding overlap and minimizing any risk of cannibalization. Instead, it complements existing structures by capturing demand that falls outside tightly controlled targeting.

The result is additional revenue at a marginal cost - achieved not by increasing spend, but by improving coverage across the full demand curve.
