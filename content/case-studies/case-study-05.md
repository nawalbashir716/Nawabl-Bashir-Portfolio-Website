Title: Inside a Seasonal Launch - Selling Out a Valentine’s Product with 23%+ Margin 

Snapshot

574 Units Sold

&#x20;$6.5K+ Revenue

&#x20;\~21% ACoS

&#x20;4.2% TACoS

&#x20;23%+ Profit Margin



Problem

Seasonal launches operate under a compressed timeline, where both demand and competition peak simultaneously.

In this case, the product was introduced into a Valentine’s Day gifting category, where most competitors concentrate heavily on broad, high-volume keywords. This typically drives up CPCs while lowering conversion rates, making profitability difficult to achieve within a short selling window.

Unlike evergreen products, there is limited room for testing or inefficiency. Missed timing or poor keyword positioning can result in unsold inventory and lost seasonal demand.

The challenge was to generate velocity quickly while maintaining control over spend, ensuring the product ranked and converted within a narrow timeframe.



Solution

The approach focused on precision rather than volume.

Instead of competing aggressively on broad “gift” or category-level keywords, the strategy prioritized high-intent, highly relevant search terms where conversion rates would be strongest. This allowed the product to gain traction without entering inflated auctions.

A tightly structured keyword framework was developed, targeting the most relevant purchase-driven queries while filtering out low-quality traffic early. This ensured spend was directed toward demand with the highest likelihood of conversion.

Listing optimization supported this strategy, with frontend and backend SEO aligned around high-intent keywords to maximize organic visibility during the peak window.

Campaign structure was kept lean and controlled. Rather than scaling spend aggressively, bids were calibrated to maintain efficiency while gradually building ranking momentum.

The objective was clear:

&#x20;capture the right demand, not all demand.



Results

The product achieved strong sell-through within the seasonal window while maintaining profitability.

A total of 574 units were sold, generating over $6.5K in revenue with a 23%+ profit margin. Advertising remained controlled, with ACoS at \~21% and TACoS at just 4.2%.

Performance was driven by high conversion efficiency rather than aggressive spend, allowing the product to scale without the typical inefficiencies seen in seasonal launches.

The result was a clean, profitable launch in a highly competitive and time-constrained category.
