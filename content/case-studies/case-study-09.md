Title

Turning AMC into a Profit Driver - 2.3x ROAS Lift by Fixing Spend Concentration



Snapshot

+132% ROAS Improvement (1.31 → 3.05)

&#x20;+5–12x Increase in Audience Spend Share

&#x20;30% Significant CPC Reduction

&#x20;95–98% Non-Branded Conversions



Problem

Initial AMC activation through Sponsored Products auto campaigns showed underwhelming performance. While high-value audiences (High LTV and SNS Lookalike) were correctly built and activated, spend and clicks were heavily diluted across broader traffic instead of being concentrated on the intended segments. This led to inefficient spend allocation and weak ROAS, not due to audience quality, but due to lack of control over where the budget was actually flowing.

Sponsored Display was also tested but proved inefficient, spending $500+ for just 2 sales. The core issue became clear — without forcing spend toward AMC audiences, the setup behaves like a standard auto campaign with minimal audience impact.



Solution

The structure was adjusted to shift control away from generic auctions and toward AMC audiences. Base bids were reduced significantly (down to \~$0.10–$0.30 depending on targeting type) to limit exposure to broad traffic. At the same time, aggression was moved into audience bid modifiers, scaling them up to \~200%–500%+ to prioritize visibility specifically for AMC segments.

This effectively reweighted the auction dynamics — instead of competing broadly, the campaigns became selectively aggressive only when AMC audiences were present. The goal was not to increase spend, but to improve spend quality and concentration.



Results

Performance improved immediately after restructuring. For High LTV audiences, ROAS increased from 1.31 to 3.05, while spend concentration shifted from just 3.9% ($15 / $388) to 47.1% ($57 / $121). For SNS Lookalike audiences, ROAS improved from 0.78 to 1.96, with spend share increasing from 33.1% to 44%.

Overall spend and impressions decreased, but efficiency improved significantly as a much larger share of budget was directed toward high-value segments. CPCs declined due to reduced exposure to competitive auctions.

An additional outcome was at the query level — over the last 7 days, \~95–98% of converting search terms were non-branded, indicating that the setup is expanding beyond branded demand and unlocking new discovery opportunities.

The result is a more controlled and efficient system where AMC audiences are not just activated, but actually prioritized in spend and performance.
