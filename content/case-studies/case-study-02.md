Problem

The account operated at significant scale, with over 10,000 active SKUs across a highly complex catalog. Managing performance at this level introduced structural challenges beyond standard campaign optimization.

There was no consistent framework for prioritizing spend across parent ASINs. Budget distribution was uneven, with certain products overspending relative to inventory while others remained underutilized despite strong demand.

Campaign structure had expanded over time without standardization, limiting visibility into performance at a parent level. As a result, it became increasingly difficult to track efficiency, control spend, and identify scalable opportunities across the catalog.

At this scale, inefficiencies compound quickly. The challenge was to introduce clarity, control, and scalability into an already complex system.



Solution

The approach centered on building a scalable system for managing performance at the parent ASIN level.

The account structure was reorganized to align campaigns, portfolios, and naming conventions with parent-level visibility. This created a consistent framework where spend, sales, and efficiency could be tracked and acted upon across thousands of SKUs.

A prioritization model was introduced across parent ASINs, factoring in demand, inventory position, and efficiency thresholds. This enabled controlled decision-making at scale:

&#x20;allocating budget toward high-potential products while pulling back from areas with limited inventory or declining returns.

Custom tracking systems were developed to monitor performance across hundreds of parent ASINs simultaneously. This allowed for proactive identification of both inefficiencies and growth opportunities, which would otherwise be difficult to surface in a catalog of this size.

Seasonality and product lifecycle were incorporated into spend allocation, ensuring the account captured peak demand periods while maintaining stability during slower cycles.

The result was a structured system capable of handling scale without sacrificing control.



Results

The account delivered sustained growth across both scale and efficiency.

In 2025, revenue increased by over 50% year-over-year, supported by improved allocation of spend and better visibility across the catalog.

In 2026, growth accelerated further. Year-to-date revenue reached $7.2M+, representing a 70.7% increase compared to the same period last year.

At the current trajectory, the brand is positioned to significantly exceed prior annual performance, with a scalable system in place to support continued growth across an increasingly complex catalog.
