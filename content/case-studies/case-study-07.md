Title: What 53% GMV Growth Looks Like on Walmart - Scaling While Holding \~6% TACOS





Overview

This case study covers a Walmart account taken over in July, with the objective of improving both top-line growth and advertising efficiency within a relatively constrained ad ecosystem.

The analysis compares Q3 (Jul–Sep) performance against Q2 (Apr–Jun), highlighting the impact of strategic restructuring, budget reallocation, and targeting optimization.

Despite Walmart’s limited campaign controls compared to Amazon, the account delivered significant quarter-over-quarter growth across both organic and paid channels.



The Challenge

Walmart presents a fundamentally different environment compared to Amazon, with several structural limitations:

Limited campaign control (no negatives, fewer optimization levers)

Lower conversion rates due to weaker traffic quality

Restricted targeting capabilities

Less mature algorithm and reporting infrastructure

At takeover, the account lacked clear budget prioritization, and spend was distributed inefficiently across products and campaigns without a strong performance-driven structure.

The key challenge was to drive aggressive growth while operating within these platform constraints, without compromising efficiency.



Strategy \& Execution

1\. Performance-Based Budget Reallocation

Budgets were aggressively shifted toward:

High-converting SKUs

Products with stable inventory levels

Campaigns demonstrating consistent ROAS

This ensured capital was deployed where it could scale profitably, rather than spread thin across the catalog.



2\. Precision Targeting Within Limited Controls

Given the inability to negate or heavily refine targeting:

Extensive keyword and competitor research was conducted upfront

Targeting was built to be as relevant and conversion-focused as possible from day one

Low-intent traffic was minimized through tighter campaign structuring



3\. Campaign Structure Simplification

Instead of over-fragmentation:

Campaigns were structured around clear intent clusters

Focus remained on signal clarity rather than volume

Reduced internal competition and inefficient spend overlap



4\. Inventory-Aligned Scaling

Scaling decisions were tightly aligned with inventory:

High-velocity SKUs were prioritized for growth

Spend was reduced on products with limited stock runway

Prevented revenue loss from stockouts and wasted ad spend



5\. Continuous Performance Auditing

Ongoing deep-dives into:

Campaign-level efficiency

SKU-level profitability

Spend vs. contribution analysis

This ensured fast iteration despite limited platform flexibility.



Results (Q3 vs Q2)

Business Performance

GMV: +53.71%

Units Sold: +36.3%

Orders: +41.67%



Advertising Performance

Ad Spend: +60.8%

Ad Sales: +103%

ROAS: +26.4%
