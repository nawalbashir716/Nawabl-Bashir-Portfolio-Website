Problem

The account was operating with no real foundation and highly inefficient spend. In the month prior to takeover, $11.5K in ad spend was generating poor returns, with TACoS at 50.4% and ACoS at 65.6%.

Conversion rates were weak, organic contribution was minimal, and the account lacked any structured approach to campaign management. Spend was distributed broadly with no clear prioritization of high-intent demand, leading to significant budget leakage.

At the same time, the brand was working with a limited catalog and no historical data to guide optimization. Scaling under these conditions without first fixing efficiency would have only compounded losses.



Solution

The first phase focused entirely on control and efficiency.

I rebuilt the campaign structure from the ground up, removing wasted spend and segmenting campaigns based on intent, match type, and product focus. Search term data was cleaned aggressively, with poor-performing queries cut early to stop leakage.

The budget was reallocated toward high-converting segments, while bids were adjusted based on real performance signals rather than broad scaling. This allowed spend to move away from inefficient traffic and into controlled, high-intent demand.

Once efficiency stabilized, the next phase was scaling.

With only two parent ASINs, growth required precision. I expanded coverage through structured keyword research, competitor mapping, and niche targeting, gradually opening up new demand pockets instead of scaling blindly.

New campaigns were launched in layers, ensuring each expansion was validated before increasing spend. As inventory increased with new variations and sizes, the structure allowed us to scale aggressively without losing control on efficiency.

The approach remained consistent throughout:

&#x20;build control first, then scale only where performance justified it.



Results

Results

The turnaround was immediate.

Within the first month, TACoS dropped from 50.4% to 18.5%, while ACoS improved from 65.6% to 27.1%. At the same time, total revenue scaled to $47.3K, marking a significant improvement in both efficiency and output.

Following stabilization, the account entered a structured scaling phase. Despite early inventory limitations, demand continued to build, supported by a strong campaign foundation.

As inventory expanded, the account scaled rapidly:

&#x20;$83K → $241K → $367K in monthly revenue, driven by disciplined campaign expansion and consistent optimization.

Over a 12-month period, the account generated over $2M in total revenue while maintaining strong efficiency at 11.8% TACoS and 21.6% ACoS.

Growth was not constrained by demand or performance - only by inventory availability. The system in place allowed for aggressive scaling without sacrificing profitability.
