problem: |
  The brand was already a category leader with strong baseline performance, but growth at this stage introduces a different set of challenges.

  Core demand was largely captured, and further scaling required expanding beyond existing high-intent traffic without compromising efficiency. Simply increasing spend on core keywords would lead to diminishing returns.

  At the same time, the category is driven heavily by repeat purchase behavior, making customer acquisition only one part of the equation. Long-term growth depended on converting new customers into repeat buyers and maximizing lifetime value.

  The challenge was to scale beyond saturation while maintaining control over efficiency and building a system that supports both acquisition and retention.
solution: |
  The strategy was built around two core pillars: aggressive new customer acquisition and lifetime value expansion.

  A deliberate 70/30 investment split was implemented — 70% of spend allocated toward non-branded campaigns to drive new-to-brand customer acquisition, and 30% toward branded demand capture.

  Expansion beyond core keywords was critical. Campaigns were built to target adjacent and exploratory intents within the automotive category, allowing the brand to reach new customer segments without being limited to existing demand.

  Multiple campaign types and match strategies were layered to dominate both high-intent and emerging search behavior, including scaling into broader category terms, competitor targeting, and testing new intent clusters to continuously unlock incremental demand.

  On the retention side, the focus shifted toward converting these new customers into long-term buyers. Subscribe & Save became a key growth lever — combining strong product positioning with tailored promotions to increase subscriber adoption and create a recurring revenue base. Cross-selling across product lines further strengthened customer lifetime value.
results: |
  The account delivered sustained, scalable growth at a high level of efficiency.

  Year-over-year revenue increased by over 115%, with the brand now on track to exceed $50M in annual sales. This growth was achieved while maintaining TACoS in the 12–15% range, ensuring profitability remained intact at scale.

  Customer acquisition efforts translated directly into long-term value. Subscribe & Save grew from approximately 15K to nearly 30K active subscribers within a year, significantly increasing recurring revenue.

  The result is a system that not only scales revenue, but compounds it through retention and lifetime value.
